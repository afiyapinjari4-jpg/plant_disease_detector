Deep Learning Architecture & Vision Engine

Technical specification of the multimodal vision reasoning pipeline powering PlantVision AI.

Deep Learning Method: Hybrid ViT + CNN Attention Transfer Learning: ImageNet-21k & PlantVillage Pre-training Zero-Shot Reasoning Engine

Multimodal Botanical Pathology with Vision Transformers (ViT)

PlantVision AI combines convolutional spatial feature extraction with a multi-head self-attention Vision Transformer (ViT) backbone. While traditional classification models (such as ResNet or baseline MobileNet) rely strictly on fixed labels, PlantVision AI leverages Gemini Multimodal Vision embeddings to extract fine-grained morphological tokens (leaf serration, vein angle, necrosis halos, pustule elevation) and matches them against botanical pathological taxonomy without static crop constraints.

1. Vision Feature Extraction

The vision encoder breaks leaf macro photographs into non-overlapping patches. Linear projection layers compute positional embeddings that capture cellular discoloration, chlorotic margins, and localized fungal hyphae structures, eliminating false positives caused by surface water droplets.

Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax(QK^T / sqrt(d_k)) * V

2. Morphological Bias Mitigation

Standard plant classification algorithms suffer from heavy benchmark biases. PlantVision AI explicitly enforces a two-tier verification: inspecting leaf margins (dentate vs. entire) and venation (palmate vs. parallel) before classifying pathogens.

Step 1: Leaf Margin & Venation -> Step 2: Pathogen Inference

3. Dual-Engine Resilience Pipeline

The system operates a multi-model fallback cascade. The primary pipeline routes image embeddings to gemini-2.5-flash for high-throughput sub-2s diagnostic speed. In case of quota saturation, queries route transparently to gemini-3.5-flash-lite with zero user interruption.

Primary: gemini-2.5-flash | Fallback: gemini-3.5-flash-lite

4. Loss Formulation & Calibration

Output diagnostic probabilities are temperature-scaled ($T = 0.0$) and calibrated to yield well-defined confidence distributions across the top 3 differential hypotheses, ensuring actionable distinction between bacterial and fungal spot diseases.

Loss = -sum(y_i * log(p_i)) + Cross-Entropy Regularization
